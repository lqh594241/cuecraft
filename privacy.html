Privacy · November 5, 2026

Local processing

Cuecraft processes files and inputs in your browser. The subtitle tool does not upload your content, require an account or save your inputs to local storage. Advertising and analytics have separate data practices described below. Closing or reloading clears the current project. Downloads and clipboard contents remain on your device until you remove them.

Advertising and analytics

The workspace loads a HilltopAds banner and Ahrefs Web Analytics. Your first click on Prepare subtitles, Download subtitles or Copy output also attempts to open an advertising page in a new tab, once per page load. The original tool action continues. Advertising providers, destinations and analytics may process your IP address, browser or device information, page visits and interactions; advertising may use cookies or other identifiers. This tool does not intentionally send subtitle text or file contents to these services. Third-party scripts run under their own policies, and advertising destinations have their own data practices. See HilltopAds privacy information and Ahrefs privacy information.

Hosting

Your hosting provider may process IP addresses, requested URLs and ordinary device or connection information. External links, if used, are governed by the destination site’s policies.

Your choices

You can close the banner with its × button. Browser settings and content blockers can limit third-party advertising, tracking and popups. Use only files you are comfortable opening on this device. You control which results you copy, print or download. No file content is sent to a remote processing service by this version.
