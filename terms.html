What this tool does

Local UTF-8 SRT/WebVTT parsing, conversion, timestamp shift, optional frame-rate duration scaling, negative-time trimming, overlap checking and optional shortening repair. No automatic transcription, translation or remote processing; VTT metadata/style blocks are omitted.

Results depend on your inputs, browser support and the file formats used. Review the output before publishing, printing, submitting a form, paying someone or committing material to a project. This tool is provided as a practical aid, without a guarantee of a particular outcome.
